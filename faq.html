Helpful Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Answers about our services, onboarding process, budgets, reporting, and what we need from you to do good work.

How quickly can we start getting new customers?
Timing depends on scope, access, approvals, and tracking readiness. After onboarding, we typically use the first phase to audit the account, confirm the offer and audience, prepare creative, configure measurement, and launch. Early data informs optimization; lead or sales volume is never guaranteed.
How much budget do I need for advertising?
There is no universal minimum. We recommend a test budget based on your market, average order value or customer value, conversion rate, sales capacity, and the amount of data needed to learn. Your media spend is separate from our management or production fees, and no return is guaranteed.
Do I have to provide the videos and ad images?
We can support concepting, scripts, editing, graphics, captions, and creative variations. Depending on the engagement, you may provide brand assets, product access, footage, approvals, or creator talent. The exact production scope is agreed before work begins.
How will I know if the marketing is working?
We agree on the most useful KPIs at the start, then report on spend, reach, traffic, leads, conversions, and revenue where the tracking setup supports it. Reports include context and next actions, because platform metrics alone do not tell the whole business story.
